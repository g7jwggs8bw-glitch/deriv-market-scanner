How to interpret this scanner

GREEN / STABLE means the scanner has not detected its configured warning conditions in the available sample. It does not mean the next digit is predictable or that the bot has a positive expected return. A fair independent Even/Odd process remains 50/50 on each tick regardless of recent streaks. Use the result as a data-quality and regime monitor, not as a profit signal.
